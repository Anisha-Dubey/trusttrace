          Formal Architecture & Policy Invariants
        

        
      

      

        

          
1. TLS-001 (Core Invariant)

          
Valid DV TLS certificate proves transport encryption only, never organizational legitimacy.

        

        

          
2. COV-001 (Coverage Arithmetic)

          
checks_total == checks_observed + checks_failed + checks_not_run. Zero unallocated checks permitted.

        

        

          
3. SEC-001 (Zero Egress Abuse)

          
RFC1918 private IPs, AWS/GCP metadata (169.254.169.254), and loopback targets are rejected before network egress.

        

        

          
4. SEC-002 (Prohibited Credentials)

          
URLs containing embedded credentials (user:pass@host) are rejected immediately to prevent credential leaks.

        

        

          
5. EVID-002 (Negative Threat Match)

          
Absence of a blacklist match produces no_threat_observed, NOT guaranteed benign status.

        

        

          
6. SAFE-001 (Zero Active Browser Risk)

          
Metadata collection only. Never executes arbitrary DOM, JavaScript, or flash payload on untrusted targets.

        

        

          
7. COMM-001 (XSS Sanitization & Sybil Guard)

          
All community submissions are strictly sanitized, and repeat reports from the same source do not inflate confidence.

        

        

          
8. SCH-001 (Audit Trail Traceability)

          
Every finding points to at least one concrete evidence record ID with source reliability metadata.
