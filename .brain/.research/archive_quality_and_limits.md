## Material quality cautions inherited from the audit
The audit says the earlier report’s underlying research stream files were not present in the uploaded package and therefore some claims about those streams could not be verified. It flags bibliographic misidentification of the DOI paper 10.1145/3545948.3545958 and arXiv:2405.05206 in the older synthesis, plus duplicated citations and broad novelty language. The later audit corrected these issues and should govern project positioning.

The audit also narrows the eMudhra incident: use primary incident records and carefully attribute causal details; do not infer public early-warning capability or unreported downstream abuse.

## Limitations of this workspace synthesis
This is an execution-oriented compression, not an exhaustive independent literature review. The relevant original sources are retained selectively. No source paper was re-evaluated in full for every claim in this workspace. Dataset access, license, exact version, label semantics and timestamps must be re-checked before experiments. No current threat-feed/API availability or terms are guaranteed.

## Selection policy
Keep project-specific technical plans and the audit in the research basis. Keep directly relevant or materially adjacent PDFs only. Retain the original inventory to preserve the team’s broader archive manifest. Excluded bulk articles remain accessible in the original uploaded archive held by the user and are not copied into this compact `.brain` bundle.
