The 8 Architectural Invariants

Every evaluation emitted by TRUSTTRACE adheres strictly to immutable mathematical proofs. No untracked checks, no fake authority from transport locks, and zero active browser exploitation surface.

1. INV-TLS-001 CORE THESIS
Valid DV TLS != Identity Verified

Domain-Validated certificates prove transport channel encryption between client and endpoint. They provide zero proof that the entity operating the domain is authorized to represent the claimed institution.

Enforced in: policy/integrity_auditor.py
2. INV-EVID-002 EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE
Negative Lookup != Proof of Safety

Absence of a blacklist entry is non-evidence of benevolence. Reports containing negative matches must carry explicit non-absolute safety disclaimers and prohibit "safe" verdicts.

Enforced in: policy/signal_extractor.py
3. INV-COV-001 COVERAGE MATH
Total Checks = Observed + Failed + Not Run

Coverage arithmetic must be mathematically closed. A check that was not run due to budget exhaustion or safety gating cannot disappear from the denominator.

Enforced in: policy/input_validation.py
4. INV-CLAIM-001 TRUTHFULNESS
Uncalibrated Pseudo-Probabilities Prohibited

No synthetic percentage scores (e.g., "99.4% safe"). Confidence is strictly ordinal categories (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW, NEGLIGIBLE) derived from structural coverage and freshness policies.

Enforced in: policy/uncertainty.py
5. INV-FINDING-001 PROVENANCE
Zero Dangling Findings / Strict Traceability

Every policy finding must reference one or more genuine evidence record IDs recorded during intake. Fabricated findings without backing telemetry fail pre-publication audit.

Enforced in: policy/triage_engine.py
6. INV-COV-002 ABSTENTION GATE
Minimum Coverage Threshold for Definitive Verdict

If required checks fail or timeout, the engine must abstain or cap confidence at inconclusive with an explicit plain-language Abstention Notice explaining the deficiency.

Enforced in: policy/uncertainty.py
7. INV-COMM-001 SYBIL RESISTANCE
Independent Corroboration Across Source Families

Repeated submissions from the same reporter or upstream feed provider family do not inflate the independent verification count. Verification requires independent corroboration.

Enforced in: evidence/community/
8. INV-SEC-001 BOUNDARY DEFENSE
Zero Egress on SSRF / Embedded Credentials

Loopback IPs (127.0.0.1), RFC1918 subnets, cloud metadata (169.254.169.254), and credential-embedded URLs are halted at the intake gate before any network packet is dispatched.

Enforced in: collectors/intake.py
